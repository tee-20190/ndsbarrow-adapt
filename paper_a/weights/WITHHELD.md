# What this deposit does not contain, and why

**Never published (privacy and site protection):**
- register geometry, record identifiers and footprints from any register (Denkmalatlas Niedersachsen, NHLE, Fund og
  Fortidsminder, RCE);
- proposal coordinates, including matched and unmatched proposals. Unmatched proposals may mark unrecorded sites;
- definitions of sealed or reserved test areas, evaluation-core lists, cross-fitting fold assignments and training
  window tables;
- terrain rasters. These are public at source (LGLN, Environment Agency, AHN, SDFI) and can be fetched there.

**Weights not in this deposit:**

| Item | Why | Where it is |
|---|---|---|
| Public ADAF barrow and AO weights | upstream work | Zenodo 15848663 (CC BY-SA 4.0) |
| DK-REGSUP-1 arm A, 45 checkpoints (15 seeds × 3 folds) | trained partly on Fund og Fortidsminder geometry; the geometry licence is not stated, so the agency is asked first | author's private archive; SHA-256 in the project registry |
| CONTEXT-RESCUE-1 context arms (CR1C, seeds 05–07) | Supplement S2 negative result; the files are 640.7 MB each, about twice a plain checkpoint, and are not yet in the project's checkpoint registry | author's private archive; on request |
| Pooled-SSL encoders (SSL_G_101–303) and the SSL encoders of seeds 404–1515 | training intermediates; the fine-tuned models built from them are deposited | author's private archive; on request |
| D1 package AO-class runs and other v1 checkpoints | not used in this paper | preprint v1 weights deposit (Zenodo draft 23267481, not yet published) |
