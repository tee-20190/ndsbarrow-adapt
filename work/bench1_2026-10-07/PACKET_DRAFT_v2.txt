4. WHAT IS COMPARED

P[X,k,r] = FRESH-1 benchmark primary (barrow-group recall averaged over 0.5, 1, 2 background proposals per km2;
region_score.py and hier_uncertainty.py byte-identical; reach 15 m; NMS 60 m). Also reported at 4 per km2.
  m_X,r   = mean over the 5 seeds of P[X,k,r]
  Cmp_r   = max(m_BARROW_L,r , m_AO_L,r), re-selected inside every bootstrap draw
  Delta_r = m_J_L,r - Cmp_r ;  Delta = (Delta_R1 + Delta_R2) / 2


5. ANALYSIS AND VERDICT (fixed before any output)

5.1 Bootstrap: 20,000 draws; SeedSequence(20261007).spawn(4) = [R1 seeds, R1 clusters, R2 seeds, R2 clusters], all
  independent. R1 and R2 models are separately trained, so seed indices are drawn independently per region; within
  a region one seed-index vector per draw is shared by the three arms (paired seeds). 2 km clusters resampled within
  each region. Seed-variance correction: in each draw an arm's mean is a + sqrt(K/(K-1)) x (s - a), where a is the
  all-seed mean and s the seed-resampled mean on the draw's clusters (K = 5, factor 1.118). 95 % percentile interval.

5.2 Verdict:
  ADVANCE-both       Delta >= +0.040, 95 % lower bound > 0, every Delta_r > 0 (point), no collapsed run in any arm.
  ADVANCE-pooled     Delta >= +0.040, lower bound > 0, every Delta_r >= -0.030, no collapse, but some Delta_r <= 0.
  INFERIOR           95 % upper bound of Delta < 0.
  NOT_DISTINGUISHED  otherwise.
  Only ADVANCE-both counts toward the roadmap's "Distinctive" level. Per-region labels are reported beside it.

5.3 Minimum effect +0.040, tied to the D3 decision. D3 switches its default start from the public, citable ADAF
  barrow start to our lineage only for a gain worth maintaining a project start: +0.04 recall at fixed burden is
  about 12 more register groups per 300-group district at the same review load (about +6-7 % relative on the
  adapted level of ~0.6-0.7). It is not a noise floor: 0.037 (HR1 same-seed rerun) and JOINT-2's 1.1-1.5 points are
  single-run differences; for 5-seed means the seed noise is smaller (D1 F1 seed SD 0.011-0.020 per run, about
  0.005-0.009 for a 5-seed mean). A lower bound > 0 with Delta < 0.040 is reported as "positive, below the decision
  threshold" (NOT_DISTINGUISHED).

5.4 Collapse. Conditions (a) and (c) are D1's; (b) is changed from D1, where each arm had its own reference.
  (a) zero proposals on the region's evaluation manifest;
  (b) primary < half of the best zero-shot reference in that region (max of S*, ADAF barrow, ADAF AO zero-shot
      primaries), the same threshold for every arm;
  (c) online detector ON at the end.
  Collapsed runs are scored as they are; no redraw, no drop. Any collapse blocks both ADVANCE labels.

5.5 Recipe reliability (reported, not part of the label): per region, the seeds k with
  P[J_L,k] > max(P[BARROW_L,k], P[AO_L,k]), out of 5.

5.6 Feasibility (power_sim_v2.py -> POWER_SIM_v2.json; 300 datasets x 1,000 draws per cell; model-based, not a
  forecast). Complete rule above; displacement (J_L loses comparator hits at rate b, gains at b + delta, cluster SD
  0.08); independent per-region seed streams; seed term widened; flat-barrow stratum (R1 34 %, R2 assumed 20 %) hit

  hit 0.75; AO_L 0.04 below BARROW_L; seed SD BARROW_L 0.012, AO_L 0.020, J_L 0.02 or 0.05; b 0.05 or 0.10;

  One region (R1-only fallback), tau 0: null false win 0.013-0.053 after the fix (v1, before the fix: 0.023-0.117);
